# Changelog

## v0.1.0

Not released yet.

- **Blizzard's quest tracker in liquid glass**: the reflective glass the other Glass windows use
  (LibGlass r9). Blizzard's own tracker keeps working underneath, so Edit Mode still moves it and
  sets its height and text size. The glass hugs the quests you track, and shrinks to the header
  when the tracker is collapsed. Smoked glass lit from above: a bright rim along the top, a
  hairline and a soft reflection over the header, a quiet bottom.
- **Four looks:** clear, smoked or class-coloured glass, or Blizzard's own tracker back exactly as
  it ships (`/gqt theme`). A backing under the glass keeps the text readable over bright ground
  (`/gqt opacity light|medium|solid`).
