# Changelog

## v0.1.0

Not released yet.

- **Blizzard's quest tracker in liquid glass**, on LibGlass r6's clean material. Blizzard's own
  tracker keeps working underneath, so Edit Mode still moves it and sets its height and text size.
  The glass hugs the quests you track, and shrinks to the header when the tracker is collapsed.
  Charcoal glass with a fine edge: a thin bright line along the top, a darker one along the bottom.
- **Four looks:** clear, smoked or class-coloured glass, or Blizzard's own tracker back exactly as
  it ships (`/gqt theme`). A backing under the glass keeps the text readable over bright ground
  (`/gqt opacity light|medium|solid`).
